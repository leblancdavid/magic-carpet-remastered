## Storage-Backed Energy And Tier Foundation

- Separate initial match energy from resource-delivery updates. Runtime-generated worlds may add the ship before its base or vice versa; initialize energy once when starting home storage is available, then only raise/clamp limits on subsequent deliveries/spending. An initially empty base must not get a refill when its first cargo arrives.
- Explicit progression cannot be derived from storage: construction payment itself lowers storage, so storage-driven visuals would silently demote a purchased base. Guard sequential completion with an expected target tier to reject skipped or duplicate callbacks.
- Capacity can fill while a drone is travelling. Gate both seeking and scooping, accept partial deposits and retain the undeposited owned cargo. Repeated retries must conserve it; cargo remains physical world resources exactly once even while carried. Do not neutralize overflow simply because another delivery filled the base.
- Conservation-only tests must distinguish physical transfers from intentional payments. Keep atomic spending separate from partial withdrawals, report expenditure outside physical totals, and extend the accounting with refund/project-destruction ledgers when construction is introduced. Isolate ability tests with sufficient storage-backed energy rather than relying on a removed hidden starting allowance.

- Damage-scaled storage must be continuous across destroyed-tier boundaries. Multiplying the surviving tier's whole capacity by its own remaining-HP fraction can increase capacity when a tier falls. Scale only the top layer's incremental capacity over intact lower layers, and cap quiet repair at surviving-tier maximum HP.
- Snapshot paid cost/duration and use a unique project instance ID for cancellation. A stale slot-only UI action can otherwise cancel/refund a replacement; rebuild refunds/destruction must use the reduced payment rather than looking up the current full upgrade price.
- Resolve project state and clear its slot before refund/spill side effects. Keep storage loss separate from investment loss, and split odd payments per project so spill plus permanent loss exactly equals payment. Gross payments minus refunds minus investment spills provide a net-commitment ledger alongside physical resources; active investment is not counted as loose/storage antimatter.
- Godot may replace duplicate sibling node names with internal names. Resource assertions should total all live/pending fragments by ownership and reconcile transaction ledgers rather than assume every fragment retains its source-name prefix. Radial test positions must offset `PlanetCenter`, not accidentally place an actor at the center itself.

## Economic Capture And Cargo Ownership

- Multi-resource construction must validate and debit both reserves in one transaction, snapshot each actual paid amount, and round rebuild prices/destruction splits per resource. Adding material via a second spend call risks losing antimatter when material payment fails. Material-only payments must not change storage-backed energy. Give material its own physical/committed/generated-supply identity; count attached crates once, never again as a drone aggregate.
- Collector saturation is resource-specific: full antimatter should not block eligible matter and vice versa. Shared cargo count must cover both lists in every seeking/returning/docked/transfer/destruction branch. Exercise actual radial Claim sweeps and delivery routes, mixed full/partial cargo theft, and independent storage gates, not only direct deposit helpers.
- Adding a HUD reserve can expand a Godot panel beyond its requested size through label minimum heights. Inspect rendered HUD and adjacent radar together; a readable material label can still be hidden by the radar. Set sufficient panel height and keep a visible gap before the radar.

- Keep site ownership until timed capture completes; compute research modifiers from that ownership rather than incrementing/decrementing mutable bonuses. Apply elapsed extractor work to the old owner, clear fractional work on transfer/full storage, and count only accepted deposits as new resource supply. Income is not a physical transfer or construction refund.
- Transferring a collector must change destination, groups, visual ownership and every attached cargo resource in one synchronous operation. Clear its old seek/return/dock state, keep physical cargo/amounts intact, and revalidate ownership at scoop/deposit. Test repeated theft at full/partial storage rather than only empty-drone transfers.
- Angularly mirrored globe sites can still land nearly on a base at a different world radius. Check actual opening surface distances on every preset; mirrored angles alone do not prove fair travel or terrain accessibility.
- Repeated full-world verification needs native-safe teardown and explicit process exit-code checks. This capture harness passed all assertions but initially crashed during headless shutdown and stalled during repeated-world rendered shutdown. Deferred world deletion, extra drain frames and managed-wrapper finalization before runtime shutdown resolved the headless run; use one rendered world for presentation and all-size headless layout checks. This is harness cleanup evidence, not a general engine crash diagnosis.

## Exploration Rewards And Match Modifiers

- Reveal and collection are separate transactions. Destruction gives no exclusive claim; mark salvage collected before generating a secured offer, and remove an offer before applying choice/deposit side effects. Store immutable offers with unique IDs so defer/respawn cannot reroll or allow both outcomes. Test actual swept projectile hits, racing collectors and viewport-routed choice clicks.
- Derive boon stats from match stack counts rather than mutating authored equipment/flight values. Calculate researched rank first, add stacks within one family, multiply independent damage/source factors and round damage once. Maximum-HP acquisition and healing are distinct; base-HP changes also move tier-loss boundaries and need an explicit layer policy before integration.
- Uniform spherical placement uses uniform longitude and latitude sine, not uniform latitude. Exclude every base with surface distance, check support/spacing, bound attempts/population and defer failure without allocating an actor. Balancing cumulative nearest-base regions reduces clustering but is not proof of equal travel difficulty. Verify initial/timed arrivals at poles, across seams and on every radius.
- A skip reward introduces new physical antimatter supply. Test deposit plus owned overflow against the awarded amount, including full storage; it raises the storage-backed energy limit without refilling current energy or entering construction-refund ledgers. Hidden scheduled spill fragments count immediately.

## Spherical Flight Lessons

- Give short built-in thrust and slotted impulse mobility explicit interaction rules. Surface-relative forward thrust should not inherit camera-pitch lift or add diagonal input speed; a stronger aimed Dash must end active thrust so the next movement tick cannot overwrite its impulse. Fresh-press activation through non-echo input events avoids auto-repeat or firing when held keys survive a live UI close. Verify actual solver movement on the globe underside as well as energy/timer state; inspect rendered HUD strings for wrapping beyond fixed panel height.

- Real-time modal UI needs a gameplay-input gate as well as GUI event consumption. Ship movement/equipment use global action polling, which can remain pressed even when a Control consumes the event. Keep simulation/recharge/damage live, block look/movement/casting explicitly, restore cursor ownership and wait for held combat actions to release before firing on close. Live panels must not clear setup/result pause.
- Preserve button identity and focused tab/scroll state across refreshes; update existing controls rather than rebuilding the UI every tick. Cancellation must use the displayed project's instance ID, not whichever project occupies the slot at click time.
- Viewport-routed synthetic GUI clicks use local coordinates when derived from `Control.GetGlobalRect()`. Pass `inLocalCoords=true` to `PushInput`; fullscreen stretch otherwise transforms already-local coordinates again. Tests for render-driven automatic UI closure must wait for process frames, not assume two physics ticks imply a render update.
- Research effects should be derived from completed IDs and current prerequisites without repeatedly mutating authored base values. Suspension/rebuilding restores the same multiplier once. Repeatable unit completion is a transaction, not permanent researched ownership; spawn into the owning world and guard one-shot target damage/lifetime.

- Ground and seam skirts sharing a material can share one mesh surface to reduce per-tile draws. Preserve independent skirt vertices for flat clockwise normals and keep skirts out of collision faces. Cache only scratch native-input buffers whose setters copy synchronously; published-resource independence must be verified through later builds/edits and actual ray coverage. Measure allocations after batching: a larger combined vertex layout can initially increase managed pressure, even when draw count improves. VSync-limited p95 and variable transient fine sets do not isolate GPU throughput.

- Collider publication grace is needed for live terrain edits as well as LOD swaps. Assigning a new shape resource to an enabled node can temporarily remove query coverage. Publish a sibling shape, retain the old shape through a physics step, and free edit-only retired nodes afterward; test immediate pre-physics rays and stable collision-child counts. Terrain-coverage probes must exclude live resource bodies, otherwise the first globe hit can look like a terrain failure. Mixed workloads should verify exact resources while allowing natural body-count reduction through merging.

- Dispose short-lived Godot C# collection/resource owners deterministically in terrain build loops. Assigning an ArrayMesh or collision shape to a native node retains a native reference; a temporary managed wrapper can then be disposed without removing the node's resource. Letting thousands of these owners reach finalization can defer expensive native unrefs to the main thread after GC, outside the measured rebuild callback. Verify actual rendered/collision survival, not just compilation. Compare phase wall time against quantized OS thread CPU: busy pre-ProcessFrame cleanup is different from a low-CPU presentation wait.

- Unpaced prescribed camera routes need route-step observer sampling: wall-time physics scans otherwise see different positions each lap and alter hysteresis working sets. Retain collision/settled-count guards and label added scan work. Viewport GPU timestamps exclude presentation/driver waits; low GPU time and absent GC pauses do not explain a long wall interval. Keep adjacent samples for lagging monitors and use finer phase/native tracing before assigning causality.

- Publishing replacement LOD shapes during rendering is not sufficient proof of physics query coverage in that same frame. Keep old collision through an intervening physics step, cancel pending retirement if a coarse shape is reused, and test repeated rendered traversal as well as settled seam rays. Brief overlap is preferable to a handoff hole; authoritative height queries remain necessary during edits.
- Hysteresis intentionally changes the home working set after first arrival. Compare repeated identical routes/returns when checking retirement, and distinguish live tile/node counts from native static memory, managed heap and GPU resources. Stable nodes do not prove absence of resource-lifetime stalls or memory leaks.

- Whole-world UI must project radial directions, not world X/Z. Use a fixed survey frame and seam-wrapped equirectangular UVs for the overview, and angular distance in the player's tangent heading frame for radar; round-trip both hemispheres/poles and test actual far-side destinations.
- Decouple radar motion from visibility/metadata sampling. Low-frequency redraws and actor-position snapshots visibly step even at a steady game frame rate. Track live positions of cached known actors at render cadence, guard native-node lifetime and availability, retain slower visibility scans/text updates, and calculate spherical guidance from directions without terrain sampling. Reuse draw buffers when increasing redraw cadence.
- Distributing resource drops requires compatible hauler reach. Expanding search to the whole globe solves unreachable possessed mana but introduces long balloon absence, so treat travel/delivery pacing as a separate playtest question.
- Spawn into the owning world parent rather than `SceneTree.CurrentScene` when constructing a world dynamically. CurrentScene can still be the previous scene during Ready, leaving orphan actors holding removed player/castle references on reload.
- A paused interactive map must own/release pause and mouse mode, consume modal input, refuse opening over existing menus/results, and reset scene-lifetime chart/waypoint state on restart. Test routed viewport input, not only direct method calls; global synthetic input may lack a valid window route in headless mode.
- Do not synthesize pause/menu/reload input reentrantly in a rendering post-draw callback; wait for a subsequent simulation frame after taking captures to avoid render dirty-list reentrancy.
- Grounded enemies reposition during Ready. Explicit combat-radius probes should be positioned after Ready and held still so camera-default changes do not inadvertently change the intended test geometry.

- Globe movement needs a location-dependent up vector. Apply the great-circle step rotation to up, heading, and tangential velocity together; this preserves orientation through poles without a geographic-north bias.
- Radius and speed control different aspects of comfort: angular up rate is `speed / (radius + clearance)`, while visible curvature remains a geometric property even when movement is slowed. Tune castle travel distance independently of circumference.
- Radial carpet lift can counter constant inward gravity and regulate clearance without introducing orbital mechanics. This analytic flight constraint is separate from terrain collision and rigid-body mana physics.
- A pitched chase-camera offset can enter the sphere at low clearance even when the carpet is safe; clamp camera radius independently.
- Godot headless mode does not provide captured mouse state. Test flight simulation with explicit input values rather than relying on mouse capture to activate synthetic actions.
- A collision body's linear tangent step adds outward radius drift on a globe. Preserve the solver's measured radial displacement and reproject the new location before transporting orientation/velocity; otherwise altitude slowly rises during sustained horizontal flight.
- Cube-sphere terrain edits can share a single direction-based height function and persistent stamps across all faces. Rebuild every intersecting patch, keep active collision sampling consistent across boundaries, and hide far/fine mesh gaps with visual skirts.
- Validate migrated mechanics on the underside and at face seams. World-Y assumptions can look correct near the starting pole while breaking gravity, cargo offsets, possession, respawn, and spell discoveries on the opposite hemisphere.
- Rigid mana needs global downward gravity disabled, inward acceleration applied in force integration, and radial floor/ceiling handling. Keep ordinary terrain contacts in the solver so slope rolling/merging remain physical.
- Logistics and steering need surface arc distance and tangent directions; projectiles need real straight-line aim/sweeps/visibility. Flattening enemy aim into global X/Z silently breaks combat away from the starting hemisphere.
- Godot C# wrappers remain non-null and still pass `is` checks after their native node is freed. Projectiles can outlive their caster, so use `IsInstanceValid` before native owner calls such as `GetRid`; omit the owner exclusion after disposal and let the shot continue its normal hit/lifetime behavior. Regression checks must advance past actual deferred deletion before exercising the shot.

## Phase 1 Prototype Lessons

- A playable prototype benefits from code-generated primitives and procedural content first. This keeps the game runnable without proprietary assets or asset import friction.
- Terrain height must be treated as a gameplay constraint, not just collision. Directly clamping carpet clearance against `HeightmapArena.HeightAt()` fixed terrain trapping more reliably than relying only on physics contacts.
- Spawn positions should be derived from terrain height. Placing the player, pickups, enemies, and pillars with explicit terrain clearance avoids buried or floating gameplay objects after procedural terrain changes.
- Godot scene timing matters for generated nodes. For runtime-created VFX, pickups, and projectiles, add the node to the scene tree before setting `GlobalPosition` when global transforms are required.
- Transparent water over the terrain can hide or flatten terrain readability in the prototype. Opaque/darker water with vertex-colored terrain made the island easier to read.
- The HUD is a fast tuning surface, but permanent diagnostics obscure the gameplay being tuned. Keep health/mana, objective progress, selected spells, and transient feedback in small edge panels; put speed, clearance, resource totals, role legends, and full controls behind a debug toggle.
- Flight motion should use capped acceleration/deceleration rather than uncapped interpolation weights. This avoids frame-rate-sensitive overshoot and makes tuning values easier to reason about.
- Carpet banking reads better when it follows actual local velocity instead of raw input. The visual tilt then reflects drift and deceleration, not just key presses.
- Speed-responsive camera distance and FOV add useful motion feedback before bespoke art or camera effects exist.
- Short post-hit invulnerability is necessary once enemies have contact or projectile damage. Without it, overlapping enemies can drain health too quickly and make damage feel unfair.
- Ranged enemy pressure improves the arena loop even before sophisticated AI. A windup telegraph plus slow projectile gives the player something to dodge while preserving simple enemy movement.
- VFX should communicate ownership and outcome. Orange player firebolts, purple enemy projectiles, and larger red enemy death bursts make combat events easier to parse.
- Synthesized placeholder audio is useful before original audio production. Code-generated tones give immediate feedback for flight, casting, pickups, and hits without introducing asset dependencies.

## Current Technical Constraints

- Discovery rewards should derive stronger effects from authored base tuning and per-player spell rank rather than repeatedly mutating exported values. This prevents duplicate rewards from stacking and separates exploration progression from castle-derived resource power. Initialize summoned-unit lifetime/damage before adding it to the tree, since `_Ready` snapshots lifetime.
- Scene-reload checks resumed from `PhysicsFrame` should defer their restart action to the end of the frame. Synchronous removal during that signal can leave scheduled actors trying to read detached world transforms; the deferred action models normal input timing and gives clean integration checks.

- `dotnet build MagicCarpetRemastered.sln` verifies C# compilation quickly, but it does not prove the Godot scene runs correctly.
- `godot` need not be on `PATH` for runtime verification. Godot .NET 4.6.3 is installed at `D:\Software\Godot\Godot_v4.6.3-stable_mono_win64\Godot_v4.6.3-stable_mono_win64_console.exe`; use the full path for headless imports, regression scenes, and arena smoke runs. Visual feel still requires manual playtesting.
- The prototype still favors direct systems over reusable frameworks. Keep new systems small until flight/combat feel is proven.

## Phase 2 Terrain Lessons

- A soft per-frame terrain queue budget cannot bound an atomic mesh/collision build. Rendered XL profiling measured an 87-ms batch despite a two-ms budget; bound individual work units before reducing the queue budget further. Pair per-frame rebuild telemetry (zeroed when idle) with rendered wall intervals, and separate dense-body stress from terrain impacts. Post-draw timings include frame pacing/submission, not measured GPU execution; first events after warmup are not cold-cache benchmarks.
- Split fine geometry into divisor-aligned tiles to keep the original sampling grid while reducing atomic collision work. Keep the old collider until the whole initial replacement is ready, and reverse that ordering when returning to coarse LOD. Invalidate dormant coarse vertex caches after fine edits; otherwise an unchanged-resolution downgrade can silently restore stale heights. Test actual radial collision at seams, on the underside, and through fine/coarse/fine cycles. Smaller units trade lower rebuild spikes for more nodes/draw calls, so profile traversal separately from stationary impacts.

- Deformable terrain needs one authoritative stored height grid. Mesh vertices, collision triangles, and `HeightAt()` clearance queries should all read from that grid so visual terrain, physics, and flight safety stay in sync after edits.
- Radius-based terrain edits should convert world-space bounds into grid index bounds before touching vertices. This keeps crater and sculpt operations proportional to effect size instead of arena size.
- Terrain deformation can safely update the authoritative height grid immediately and defer visual/physics mesh rebuilding. Coalescing rebuild requests avoids duplicate terrain work when multiple spells edit terrain in one frame.
- Chunked terrain rebuilds are the right next step once deferred rebuilds still feel too expensive. The chunk size becomes a gameplay/performance tuning knob, not just an implementation detail.

## Original-Spirit Design Lessons

- Mana should not behave like ordinary shooter ammunition. The original game's strategic identity comes from mana being claimed, hauled, stored, stolen, and converted into long-term power through the castle. Current mana should regenerate from the player's owned/claimed pool so spending spells creates temporary pressure without forcing constant pickup scavenging.
- The underlying design is an embodied RTS economy disguised as first-person aerial spell combat. Prioritize systems where flight, spell choice, mana ownership, balloons, castles, rivals, and terrain deformation interact without scripted events.

## Mana Ownership Loop Lessons

- Dense rigid-body hitches can come from managed neighbor logic rather than native solver/setup. Compare fresh frozen, solver-plus-custom-gravity and full-behavior fields; time synchronous injection separately from callbacks and record contact monitors. Summed callback timings overlap whole-frame timings and exclude native solver execution. Repeated support queries inside every neighbor loop multiply terrain/native-wrapper work; cache support per physics tick with movement, velocity, radius, collision-mask, water and live-terrain revision guards. Reject unavailable/pending/carried/grace-protected bodies before using cached support, and filter irrelevant body-entry callbacks before allocating deferred closures. Same-tick terrain edits must invalidate support before deferred collision rebuilds.

- Make loose mana a first-class owned object rather than a consumable. A `Neutral`/`Player`/`Enemy` ownership state per orb, distinct colors, and a shared group turn possession, hauling, and contesting into simple queries (`GetNodesInGroup("loose_mana")`) instead of bespoke per-system bookkeeping.
- Ownership should gate collection at the source. Per user direction (2026-10-10), players possess mana for balloons to haul; they never attract or absorb spheres of any color. Player-owned mana retains gravity and collisions until a balloon picks it up. Castle delivery grows spell capacity/regeneration; possession only changes ownership.
- An economy spell should be cheap and fast (low mana, short cooldown) because it kicks off a longer ownership chain. Possession now uses a visible, straight-heading terrain-following bolt rather than instant aim-weighted selection; skip carried orbs so balloons cannot be strip-mined mid-flight.
- When a unit hauls owned mana, carry the same `ManaPickup` nodes rather than destroying them on scoop. That keeps one source of truth for amount and ownership. If destruction fragments cargo into smaller globes, debit/consume the original nodes exactly once before scattering the total; do not keep both original cargo and new fragments counted.
- Resource systems benefit from a single tally node. `WorldManaTracker` sums loose/carried spheres, castle storage, and creature reserves, separating intrinsic neutral-creature mana from rival-controlled reserves. Players have no physically absorbed-mana reserve. Current playtest direction gives recurring neutral creatures intrinsic reserves: count them at spawn and conserve them on death, recognizing that those spawns introduce supply. Wizard respawns retain a zero starting reserve and cannot mint physical mana.
- When ownership is transferred on death, revert to neutral except where the enemy is the explicit contesting agent (thieves/wizards). Player-only collection pressure comes from neutral drops; rival drops must be possessed, which creates the targeted incentive to contest, not just blast everything.
- Separate physical resource accounting from rechargeable combat capacity. The older `ClaimedMana` starting-allowance/capped-power model was superseded by storage-backed energy on 2026-10-11. Recompute the limit from retained home storage after deposits, withdrawals, payments or damage rather than incrementally modifying prior derived values. Count physical antimatter in spheres, bases and enemy reserves exactly once; ship energy and its limit are never extra world resources.
- Castle economic damage needs a deterministic storage limit: intact capacity times remaining health, rounded up, with zero capacity on destruction. Spill only storage exceeding that limit, distribute integer remainders exactly, and debit storage before publishing neutral spheres. Bound the number of new bodies and place them beyond the keep's corner footprint at terrain/water plus sphere radius. Re-delivery must recover retained power rather than stack a permanent historical bonus; power-loss capacity clamps must also clamp current spell mana.
- Physical destruction bursts must preserve airborne release height. Use the spill/launch path that marks surface initialization complete; otherwise the pickup's first tick grounds the fragments and silently cancels their visible ballistic arc. Give outward and upward velocity, small exact-remainder fragments, and a short merge grace so immediately touching same-owner fragments do not recombine at the source. Cache the arena before the first integration step so the shared ceiling applies immediately.
- Auto-repair restores structure/capacity, not resources. Accumulate repair only for the portion of a tick after the last-hit quiet delay, reset delay/fractional repair on damage, and never repair a destroyed castle. Recovered mana must still return through possession and balloon delivery.
- Transfers must debit the source by exactly the amount accepted at the destination. Zero orb amounts immediately and exclude queued-for-deletion nodes so two consumers in one frame cannot absorb the same orb. Distribute integer remainders across death drops rather than truncating them.
- Movement and interaction checks must use the same target point. A balloon flying toward a hover-offset target cannot dock by checking a smaller radius around the ground-level target. Clamp motion steps to prevent overshoot, and do not add hover height repeatedly when returning to an already elevated anchor.
- Orient moving nodes before a capped `MoveToward` step: the step may reach its target exactly, so calling `LookAt(target)` afterward uses coincident origin/target positions. Keep arrival and vertical-only facing guards, and regression-check orientation as well as position; engine errors do not necessarily throw C# exceptions.
- Physical mana uses sphere radius as surface clearance, not an arbitrary hover offset. Keep mesh/collider synchronized. Accepted value scaling uses an exponent of 0.65; tune overall size independently (current radius `(0.35 / 3) * amount^0.65`) to preserve those ratios. Mass still follows amount. Ground clearance must take priority over the flight ceiling for huge reserves. Freeze and disable collision while hauled, then restore physics on spill.
- Animate merge presentation separately from resource accounting: consume the donor and commit exact combined mana once, then ease the survivor's actual mesh/collider radius toward its target and lift by each incremental radius change. Expose actual versus target radius so support, possession, and collisions use the current physical size. Prevent another merge during growth, retain direct resizing for partial transfers, and update growth before cargo-follow logic. Random explosion fragments should be positive integer partitions of the exact reserve, with a body cap; sorted unique random cuts avoid loops proportional to resource amount. Guarantee size variation when possible instead of occasionally rolling another equal-sized burst.
- Compact death drops require both zero launch impulse and compact initial placement. Removing velocity alone still leaves a wide ring if the old burst radius/offsets remain. Pack only enough to avoid collider overlap; preserve explicit structure exclusion distances for castle spills. On spherical worlds, straight down means local radial gravity, not negative world Y. Verify the actual airborne trajectory before ground collision/magnetism can introduce motion. Merge responsiveness includes both contact dwell and growth; current playtest tuning is 1.25 seconds plus 0.6 seconds.
- Staggered drops must reserve the entire resource total immediately instead of introducing mana on each emission tick. Hidden/frozen pending fragments can remain in accounting while `IsAvailable` and transfer gates exclude them from gameplay. Physics countdowns inherit pause and outlive the dead source when attached to its world parent; start scatter merge grace on actual release. Recheck release clearance against current active spheres because earlier fragments can grow or roll into previously safe spots; lift vertically rather than launching sideways. Verify partial-release totals, pending collection rejection, pause/resume, and final release. Check castle exclusion against the actual box footprint after attraction rather than an artificial enclosing circle.
- Cluster merging needs per-pair continuous contact dwell and reliable support detection. Absolute linear/rotational speed gates silently disable magnetism and merging on slopes; use relative pair velocity for contact dwell and limit only added attraction speed toward the neighbor. Solver floor manifolds can disappear while balls press together: retain support through terrain clearance or a short floor ray and tolerate a small contact skin. Nearest-neighbor attraction avoids cancellation in dense fields; use enough tangential acceleration/range for visible convergence. Verify separated balls actually approach AND eventually merge without test-side teleportation, on flat and spherical surfaces. Reset after separation, carry, ownership mismatch, and survivor growth; revalidate deferred merges. Enlarged bursts need initial fragment separation checks. Dispose temporary ray-query resources to avoid Godot C# finalizer errors at shutdown.
- Defer rigid-body contact mutations (shape resizing, merging, absorption) out of contact signals. Choose a stable merge survivor, debit the donor immediately, and revalidate availability/ownership on every callback to conserve finite mana across reciprocal and chained contacts. Check sustained contacts when ownership can change after initial contact.
- Godot uses clockwise front-face winding. Double-sided terrain rendering can conceal reversed concave collision faces, while height-based flight clearance conceals the missing physics contact. Check real rolling bodies on the actual heightmap, not only on synthetic box slopes. Leave normal terrain contact penetration to the solver; only correct significant terrain burial or support collider-free water, otherwise height snapping cancels downhill motion.
- Ground-skimming possession requires reachable globe placement: loose globes touch terrain/water while carried globes follow their hauler. Sample the live height grid in short projectile substeps and sweep each segment against each globe's current sphere radius plus bolt tolerance; deferred terrain collision rebuilds and endpoint-only hits are unreliable for fast skimming bolts. Keep horizontal heading fixed instead of steering toward a selected globe.
- Headless regression scenes can exercise actual Godot nodes, world tallies, carry state, and transfers without introducing a mock gameplay framework. `tools/ManaLoopChecks.tscn` covers transfer invariants; arena smoke runs cover startup/normal processing; neither proves flight feel or visual readability.

## UI Readability Lessons

- Container-based, padded panels with bounded widths and word wrapping are more readable over terrain than a single unbounded multiline label. Keep the normal HUD away from the aiming area and hide detailed diagnostics by default.
- Live-updating scrollable debug text must preserve its scroll offset, and key-repeat events must not repeatedly flip a toggle. Passive HUD controls should ignore mouse input so the overlay does not absorb spell clicks.
- Rendered screenshots complement headless input checks: the HUD screenshot review caught objective copy wrapping onto a third line and touching the debug panel. Shorten gameplay copy before expanding panel coverage.

## Level Objective Lessons

- User-directed victory is rival castle destruction AND rival wizard defeat, independent of mana ownership. Track one designated rival lifecycle rather than counting an ecosystem role. Record death before queued deletion and attach the same tracking callback to replacements. Current castle-backed respawns clear that defeat after ten seconds while the castle survives, so killing the wizard first works only until it respawns. Destroying its castle prevents future replacement.
- Wizard death must not unconditionally reload the arena. Keep dead players in place with flight/casting/collision disabled, tick recovery in the pausable objective while their castle survives, then restore health/current spell mana, collision, and brief protection beside the keep. A home-castle loss cancels recovery and remains terminal defeat. Validate actual elapsed physics frames as well as direct timer calls and paused countdown behavior.
- Keep the gameplay root pausable and the result HUD always-processing. An always-processing gameplay root would let newly spawned enemies/projectiles inherit processing while the result is displayed. Handle restart input in the live HUD during pause, unpause before reload, and mark input handled before scene reload detaches the old HUD.
- Test actual scene reload as well as objective flags. A surviving harness must live outside the reloaded `CurrentScene`; assign a current scene only after it is attached to the tree root. Headless mouse capture is not representative, so verify mouse recapture with a rendered regression run.

## Flying Rival Lessons

- Never normalize a combined steering vector that uses a tiny vertical bias to mean hover. When horizontal movement stops, that bias becomes full-speed ascent. Use planar steering plus proportional, speed-limited height correction against the live terrain/water surface, with an explicit altitude band and terrain burial protection.
- Apply the altitude rule to every flying actor, not only wizards: swarms/casters had the same normalization defect. Share terrain/water-relative ceiling queries across players, creatures, guardians, balloons, and physical mana. Clamp upward velocity along with position so holding ascend or dashing upward cannot accumulate pressure at the ceiling. Projectiles must ignore this movement ceiling: camera/muzzle offsets can already put their starting position above it, so altitude-based expiry makes casting from ceiling height fail. Preserve launch direction and ordinary hit/range/lifetime rules; test horizontal, upward, and downward shots from both ceiling height and an above-ceiling muzzle.
- A resource-seeking unit must approach within its actual absorption radius. Stopping eight units from a globe while absorption requires 2.7 units leaves the wizard idle and climbing forever. Real physics-frame sampling catches altitude drift and collection failures that startup/existence tests miss.
- Objective-critical enemies need recognizable silhouettes. A purple sphere reads as another monster or resource. Original primitive robe/hat/carpet geometry, a matching hit collider, and a world label make the rival identifiable without an asset dependency. Verify both default-camera and close-up rendered views.
## Combat Optimization Lessons (2026-10-10)

- A deferred call still runs on the main thread: deferring an entire set of mesh/collider rebuilds does not spread its cost across frames. Use a coalescing frame queue, and explicitly treat the budget as soft when one rebuild is atomic.
- Share immutable primitive meshes/materials. Animate mana visual scale instead of changing procedural sphere dimensions each physics tick; verify effective visual radius against collision radius.
- Ownership recoloring can select a shared material; simultaneous fading effects need independent materials, even when their mesh is shared.
- Per-orb group scans amplify managed/native boundary overhead and repeated support checks. Share a frame snapshot and broad-phase spatial candidates while retaining exact gameplay narrow-phase checks. Account for huge radii, negative coordinates, disposed objects and movement/teleports.
- CPU microbenchmarks identify removed work; they do not establish rendered hitch elimination. Separate index construction, physics, GPU first-use work, frame distributions and hands-on acceptance. Staggered drop release does not imply staggered body creation.
