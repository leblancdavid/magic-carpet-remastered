## Implemented controls and presentation

- `EquipmentBar` has four 88×98 cards in one row, original glyphs, labels 1–4, Roman rank/energy cost, cyan selected outline, Ready/Empty/Down text, real cooldown shading/countdown and amber energy-shortfall state. Unaffordable cards stay selectable. 1–4 selects, LMB activates, Tab cycles; cursor-visible mouse-down selects only and latches combat until release.
- Claim/Shift cost/readiness are outside the slots. Bar and B/M/H row hide behind interfaces/setup/results; pending count and damage badge use that row. Narrow canvases move it above the bar. Old separate hints and verbose loadout text are removed.
- Vitals show HP/energy, base tier/HP and A/M reserves; shield/respawn text is contextual. A = antimatter, M = matter, E = energy. Details remain in management/tooltips/F3. The short F3 hint avoids wrapping.
- Radar is a 196×224 panel with 72-pixel radius, 220 m sensing/range and two-line guidance. Both maps show home/owned capture points globally, remembered discovered rival-base locations, and other actors only inside ship sensing. Neutral mobs/antimatter/matter use black/orange/gray dots; claimed resources use faction colors. Radar hides behind other panels/setup/results.
- B retains five categories/concurrent summaries. Cancel appears only for an active instance, with exact refund/siege tooltip. Tiles show short name/rank, A/M/time and Research/Locked/Owned/Completed/Suspended/progress. Tooltips include full effects, costs, human-readable prerequisites, exact unavailable reason and retention/suspension, including disabled tiles.
- Ship upgrade rows include **Equip** toggles and numbered equipped state, with no separate Loadout tab. A fifth item is unavailable until another is disabled. Keys compact in row order, preserving selected equipment identity. Home range, pause/death gates and ownership remain; refresh preserves controls/scroll. **Apply rank** appears for equipped items with completed unapplied research.
- General grids adapt from two to six columns. Ship Research instead preserves **Equipment / I / II / III** columns and one row per item, plus built-in Claim; Plasma I shows Starting/Owned. Minimum label widths prevent collapsed wrapped headings; vertical scrolling keeps rows aligned at 1280×720 and 960×540. `UiIcon` art requires no imported/generated assets. StyleBox overrides use scoped owners; HUD frames are reused.
- A screen-space capture bar above equipment shows stage and percentage inside a capture ring without camera aiming. Departure hides it while progress drains; contested/restoring/owned feedback uses the authoritative site state. Live panels/pause/death/results hide it. World capture text/map details remain. Wreck/salvage labels are shorter within 55 units; discovery stays 120 and collection rules stay unchanged.
